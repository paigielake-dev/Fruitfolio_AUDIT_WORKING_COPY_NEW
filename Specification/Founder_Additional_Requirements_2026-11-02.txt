1. FRUIT BASKET — REALISTIC PUDDLE SOUND EFFECTS

Implement soft, realistic puddle sound effects using Fruitfolio’s existing audio architecture.

The puddle is an obstacle that creates temporary basket inertia.

The sound must correspond to actual physical interaction.

Required behavior

* When the basket enters a puddle, play a subtle water-entry sound.
* While the basket moves through the puddle, use appropriate gentle splashing or water-movement audio.
* When the basket exits, end the movement sound naturally.
* Do not play puddle audio when the basket is not interacting with a puddle.
* Do not continuously restart or stack identical sounds.
* Avoid loud, harsh, or exaggerated splash effects.
* Respect the existing sound-effects volume slider.
* Respect mute and relevant system audio settings.
* Stop or pause effects appropriately when gameplay pauses, exits, or backgrounds.
* Prevent retained audio playback after the game owner is destroyed.

Physics synchronization

The existing puddle mechanics must remain intact.

Basket inertia applies only while the basket physically overlaps the puddle.

Normal movement resumes after exiting.

Synchronize sound playback with actual collision and movement states rather than arbitrary timers.

Use existing approved audio assets where available. If no suitable sound exists, implement the integration and identify the missing asset explicitly rather than silently substituting an unrelated sound.

Verification

Test puddle entry, movement, exit, pause, resume, re-entry, and overlapping interactions.

Verify that sound does not continue after the interaction ends.

Preserve existing physics and scoring rules.

4. RESEARCH LIBRARY — SAFE, FOUNDER-MAINTAINABLE UPDATE ARCHITECTURE

Design and implement a maintainable research-update system so the founder can add, correct, replace, or retire research content without modifying Fruit Basket gameplay code.

The architecture must preserve the existing Research Library-first evidence rules and offline-first functionality.

Primary objective

I should be able to update Fruitfolio’s scientific research over time without breaking:

* Fruit Basket questions.
* Question difficulty settings.
* Existing gameplay mechanics.
* Basket Reveal facts.
* Saved citations.
* Existing encyclopedia entries.
* Previously completed rounds.
* Ask retrieval.
* Cycle Tracker educational explanations.
* Existing user data.

A. Separate research content from gameplay logic

Maintain a clear separation between:

Research content

* Research notes.
* Scientific claims.
* Supporting sources.
* Citation metadata.
* Topic classifications.
* Approved educational explanations.
* Content revisions.

Gameplay logic

* Fruit spawning.
* Fruit classification.
* Catch detection.
* Scoring.
* Difficulty mechanics.
* Obstacles.
* Star passes.
* Round completion.
* Game-state transitions.

Scientific content updates must not alter gameplay mechanics.

B. Use stable identifiers and versioned research records

Each research entry should have:

* Stable research ID.
* Content version.
* Topic classification.
* Approved scientific content.
* Supporting source identifiers.
* Citation metadata.
* Source publication details.
* Revision metadata.
* Validation status.
* Retirement or supersession status where appropriate.

Never use array positions or mutable display titles as permanent research identifiers.

Preserve historical source versions needed by saved records.

C. Provide a practical founder update workflow

Implement or document an easy-to-use update workflow.

Prefer a structured research format compatible with the existing architecture.

The founder should be able to:

1. Add a research entry.
2. Correct an existing entry.
3. Update a citation.
4. Add an approved source.
5. Retire outdated information.
6. Validate the proposed changes.
7. Preview the affected educational content.
8. Publish an approved research update.
9. Roll back a problematic update.

Avoid requiring manual edits across numerous Swift files.

If an editor is appropriate, provide a simple local editing or import workflow.

Do not introduce a public-facing research editor or unrestricted external content ingestion unless explicitly authorized.

D. Validate research before publication

Add automated checks for:

* Schema validity.
* Unique stable identifiers.
* Required source metadata.
* Citation integrity.
* Broken source references.
* Duplicate records.
* Unsupported topic mappings.
* Missing educational explanations.
* Invalid content versions.
* Unsafe or unsupported factual claims.
* Compatibility with existing saved records.

Automated checks must not be represented as independent medical or clinical approval.

Content requiring expert review must remain gated.

E. Preserve existing saved content

When research is updated:

* Existing saved citation snapshots must remain intact.
* Previously completed Basket Reveal facts must remain associated with their original evidence.
* Existing game history must remain readable.
* New questions may use the newly approved research version.
* Historical records must not be silently rewritten.
* Retired research must not break saved references.

Use explicit migration rules where needed.

F. Maintain offline-first functionality

Fruitfolio must remain usable offline.

Research updates must not require live network access during gameplay.

Use a validated local research snapshot for active sessions.

If optional update distribution is implemented, it must use an approved source and a safe installation process.

Do not add unrestricted web retrieval or automatic publication of unverified medical content.

G. Use atomic updates and rollback protection

Research updates should be validated before becoming active.

A failed update must not corrupt the existing Research Library.

Use an atomic replacement or equivalent safe activation mechanism.

Preserve the last known-good research version.

Provide a rollback path.

Do not overwrite active research with malformed or incomplete content.

H. Preserve question and round consistency

A Fruit Basket round should use a consistent research snapshot.

If research is updated during an active round:

* Do not change the current question unexpectedly.
* Do not replace its answer choices.
* Do not alter the answer key.
* Do not change its educational fact.
* Do not change its saved citation association.
* Do not modify gameplay mechanics.

Apply newly approved content to future eligible questions or sessions.

I. Provide documentation and tests

Deliver:

* Research schema documentation.
* Founder-friendly update instructions.
* Example research entry.
* Validation procedure.
* Preview procedure.
* Publication procedure.
* Rollback procedure.
* Versioning rules.
* Migration rules.
* Automated regression tests.

Test that a valid research update becomes available to new questions.

Test that an invalid update is rejected.

Test that gameplay behavior remains unchanged.

Test that offline fallback continues to work.
